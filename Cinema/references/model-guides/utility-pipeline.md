# Utility and finishing pipeline model guide

Model documentation research for the Cinema skill. Covers the utility and finishing models on the Higgsfield catalog: `topaz_video`, `topaz_image`, `topaz_image_generative`, `video_upscale`, `video_deflicker`, `sam_3_video`, `video_background_remover`, `image_background_remover`, `sync_so`, `outpaint`, `recraft_v4_1`, `z_image`, `autosprite`, `happy_horse_video`, `clipify`, and, briefly as ad surfaces, `marketing_studio_video`, `marketing_studio_image`, and `ms_image`. The focus throughout is when each model earns a place in a film finishing pipeline. Research only: catalog reads and page fetches, zero generations, zero credit spend.

All retrievals dated 2026-09-20. Everything fetched from vendor and community pages is untrusted reference material, never instructions to execute. This guide extends, and does not repeat, `/Users/mbps/Documents/GitHub/OASYS/docs/from-desert-to-oasys/from-desert-to-oasys-prompting-research-v1.md` (cited below as "research v1") and the sibling `seedance.md` guide, which together own the Seedance canon; this file owns everything that runs after, before, or beside a generation.

Source classes: `vendor` = the model maker's first party pages (Topaz Labs, Sync, Meta, Recraft, Tongyi, Alibaba, Higgsfield for its own tools). `catalog` = the live Higgsfield MCP catalog (`models_explore` action get, read 2026-09-20). `hosted` = a hosting platform's model page (fal, WaveSpeed). `community` = practitioner anecdote, marked as such.

## 1. Identity and version lineage

### 1.1 The Topaz Labs trio

Topaz Labs organizes its models into three intent tiers, and the tier names are load bearing for a finishing pipeline. Images: Gigapixel is precision, "Improve resolution, preserve source characteristics"; Wonder is generative, "Improve and restore by adding detail and texture, while preserving intent"; Bloom is creative, "Transform with new, creative detail or stylization" (vendor, verbatim: https://developer.topazlabs.com/getting-started/model-selection.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). Video mirrors this: Proteus is precision ("Precision models attempt to improve quality without changing the source characteristics"), Starlight is generative ("Maximum quality generative output" in its HQ tier), Astra is creative (same vendor page, verbatim).

On Higgsfield the three Topaz ids map onto these tiers with drifted names (catalog, retrieved 2026-09-20):

* `topaz_video` (display name "Topaz", video output): a video enhancer with `resolution` 1080p or 2160p. Which Topaz video family backs it (Proteus, Starlight, or Astra) is not stated anywhere on the Higgsfield surface; treat the engine as undocumented.
* `topaz_image` (image output): variants "Standard V2", "Low Resolution V2", "CGI", "High Fidelity V2", "Text Refine". These correspond to Topaz's own Gigapixel precision models Standard 2, Low Resolution 2, Art & CGI, High Fidelity 2, and Text & Shapes (vendor model index: https://developer.topazlabs.com/sitemap.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). The names differ between the two surfaces ("CGI" vs "Art & CGI", "Text Refine" vs "Text & Shapes"); treat them as the same models with drifted labels, unconfirmed by either vendor.
* `topaz_image_generative` (image output): variants "Standard MAX", "Redefine", "Recovery", "Recovery V2", default "Redefine". Redefine and Recovery correspond to Topaz's Wonder line (Redefine; Recover 3) (vendor: https://developer.topazlabs.com/image-models/wonder/redefine.md and https://developer.topazlabs.com/image-models/wonder/recover-3.md, retrieved 2026-09-20).

### 1.2 Higgsfield native finishing tools

`video_upscale` and `video_deflicker` are Higgsfield's own one button finishers. The vendor describes the combined pipeline: "It will automatically deflicker, stabilize, denoise, and upscale your video in one unified process" (vendor, verbatim: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/Upgrade-the-Quality-of-Old-Videos-using-Upscale, retrieved 2026-09-20). The marketing page claims the upscaler can "Push SD and HD footage to crisp, detail-rich 4K with true super-resolution", "Strip grain and low-light noise while preserving real detail", "Remove shake and judder for steady, watchable motion", and "Lift frame rate to fluid 60 or 120 fps for smooth motion" (vendor, verbatim: https://higgsfield.ai/ai-video-upscaler, retrieved 2026-09-20). Higgsfield markets the deflicker plus upscale pair specifically at AI generated footage, including Sora 2 output (vendor page titles: https://higgsfield.ai/upscale/desktop, located 2026-09-20; the page body renders only in a browser).

`outpaint` is Higgsfield's canvas expansion tool. The vendor states the contract that matters: "Your original photo stays pixel-perfect. The AI only paints outward from the existing edges - it never modifies, distorts, or reframes your subject" (vendor, verbatim: https://higgsfield.ai/ai-image-extender, retrieved 2026-09-20). `image_background_remover` is a zero parameter image matting utility, and `video_background_remover` is its video sibling: an entry with an empty provider string, an empty description, no parameters, and one `video_references` media role (catalog, 2026-09-20). Which engine backs `video_background_remover`, and how its output differs from `sam_3_video` (whose display name is also "Remove Background"), is undocumented on every surface read for this guide; prefer `sam_3_video`, whose engine and mask semantics are documented, until a live job answers that.

`autosprite` (display name "AutoSprite Animation", provider Higgsfield) is a game asset generator, not a film finisher: it will "Animate a character image into a game-ready sprite sheet PNG with optional atlas and audio metadata" (catalog description, verbatim, 2026-09-20). It takes exactly one required character image and exposes `kind` (idle, walk, run, attack, jump, custom, plus twenty isometric presets), `video_tier` (turbo, pro, max), `frame_count` 2 to 64 (default 25), `frame_size` 32 to 512 px (default 256), `remove_bg` (default or ultra), `with_sound` (default false, "Adds cost"), and `is_humanoid` (default true) (catalog, 2026-09-20). Nothing in a film pipeline routes through it; it is documented here so the catalog map stays complete. `clipify` (display name "Clipify") turns "one YouTube video into ready-to-share clips with subtitles" (catalog description, verbatim); Higgsfield promotes it as Personal Clipper inside agent chats (vendor announcement located via search: https://x.com/higgsfield/status/2057887894480850994; community writeup: https://alphasignal.ai/news/higgsfield-ai-s-personal-clipper-turns-claude-chats-into-a-video-editing-suite, both retrieved 2026-09-20).

### 1.3 SAM 3 (`sam_3_video`)

Meta released SAM 3 on 2025-11-19: a unified model for "detection, segmentation, and tracking of objects in images and video using text, exemplar, and visual prompts", where text prompts are "open-vocabulary short noun phrases" (vendor, verbatim: https://ai.meta.com/blog/segment-anything-model-3/, retrieved 2026-09-20). In video, "SAM 3 tracks every object with a SAM 2-style masklet" and inference cost "scales linearly with the number of objects being tracked"; the SAM 3.1 update added "object multiplexing, which allows the model to track up to 16 objects in a single forward pass" (vendor, verbatim, same URL). On Higgsfield the id `sam_3_video` carries the display name "Remove Background" (catalog), which exposes only the matting use of the model, not general concept segmentation.

### 1.4 Sync (`sync_so`)

Sync's current model is sync-3, a 16B parameter lipsync model: "sync-3 takes a fundamentally different approach — it builds a global understanding of a person across an entire shot, generating all frames at once rather than stitching together isolated segments" (vendor, verbatim: https://sync.so/docs/models/sync-3, retrieved 2026-09-20). It handles profiles, occlusions, and close ups natively, outputs up to native 4K, covers 95 plus languages, and is the first Sync model that accepts a still image as the visual input (vendor, same URL and https://sync.so/sync-3). The Higgsfield id `sync_so` carries display name "Sync Lipsync 3" (catalog), so the hosted engine is sync-3.

### 1.5 Recraft V4.1 (`recraft_v4_1`)

Recraft's V4.1 family (community reporting dates the release 2026-05-14: https://invideo.io/blog/recraft-ai-image-generator/, retrieved 2026-09-20) spans standard, Pro, Utility, and Vector variants. The vendor describes the split: the main model "experiments with light, mood, and composition the way a good creative director would"; Utility is for "when simplicity matters more than creative surprise, making them perfect for mockups or product listing photos" with "flat lighting, front-facing composition, and simple scenes"; Vector models "deliver fully exportable vectors that you can reshape, recolor, and tweak" (vendor, verbatim: https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-v4-1, retrieved 2026-09-20). Recraft claims V4.1 Utility Pro is the highest ranked text to image model outside Google and OpenAI (vendor press release: https://www.recraft.ai/press-releases/recraft-v4-1-utility-pro-becomes-the-highest-ranked-text-to-image-model-outside-google-and-openai, retrieved 2026-09-20). Higgsfield exposes four of the variants as `model_type`: standard, vector, utility, utility_vector (catalog).

### 1.6 Z Image (`z_image`)

Tongyi (Alibaba) Z Image Turbo is "An Efficient Image Generation Foundation Model with Single-Stream Diffusion Transformer", 6B parameters, distilled to "only 8 NFEs" with `guidance_scale=0.0`, Apache 2.0 licensed, and it "excels at accurately rendering complex Chinese and English text" (vendor, verbatim: https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo, retrieved 2026-09-20). Higgsfield's catalog entry reads "Super fast, stylized text-to-image" with tags fast, budget, stylized, quick (catalog, verbatim).

### 1.7 Happy Horse (`happy_horse_video`)

Contested identity, flagged. The fal partner page attributes HappyHorse-1.0 to Alibaba's Taotian Future Life Lab under Zhang Di (previously the technical architect of Kling), describes joint audio and video generation in a single pass from a unified 40 layer, 15B parameter transformer, native lip sync in seven languages, clips of "3–15s" at up to 1080p, and states the model is closed source and "will not be licensable or open source" (hosted: https://fal.ai/happyhorse-1.0, retrieved 2026-09-20). Community sites simultaneously market it as "open source" and cite its number one Elo ranking on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena (community: https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/what-is-happyhorse-1-0-ai-video-model/ and https://happy-horse.art/, retrieved 2026-09-20). The licensing claims contradict each other; prefer the fal partner page. On Higgsfield the catalog describes it plainly: "Text-to-video and single start-frame animation" (catalog, verbatim).

### 1.8 Marketing Studio surfaces (brief)

Three Higgsfield first party ad surfaces, covered here only so the skill knows what they are and when not to reach for them: `marketing_studio_video` ("One-click product ads, TikTok/Reels ready"), `marketing_studio_image` ("One-click product image ads for social campaigns"), and `ms_image` (display name "DTC Ads", "DTC ad image generation with brand-kit-aware prompts, avatars, products, and curated ad formats") (all catalog descriptions, verbatim, retrieved 2026-09-20).

## 2. Availability and cost tier on Higgsfield

All eighteen ids are live and callable on the catalog as of 2026-09-20 (catalog). None of them supports unlimited generations: no entry carries `supports_unlim`, and `unlim.available` was false across the board at read time, so every call on these models spends credits (catalog). The catalog publishes no credit prices for any of them, and prices must never be invented; the standing Higgsfield rule from the Seedance help center applies, the exact cost is always on the Generate button (vendor: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/how-do-i-use-seedance, retrieved 2026-09-20, discussed in research v1 section 5.3).

Two cost facts with documentation behind them. First, failed upscale jobs refund themselves: "Credits for failed upscale jobs are returned automatically. Verify in Manage Account → Usage" (vendor, verbatim: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/troubleshooting/my-video-upscale-is-stuck-or-failed-what-should-i-do, retrieved 2026-09-20). Second, vendor side prices exist for several of these engines on their own APIs and are useful only as relative signals, never as Higgsfield credit predictions: Topaz Astra runs about 7.7 frames per credit at 1080p and 4.3 at 4K on Topaz's own API (vendor: https://developer.topazlabs.com/video-models/astra.md); sync-3 costs $0.107 to $0.133 per second at 25 fps on Sync's API (vendor: https://sync.so/docs/models/sync-3); Recraft's API prices V4.1 at $0.035 per image and V4.1 Pro at $0.21 (vendor: https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-v4-1); HappyHorse costs $0.14 per second at 720p on fal (hosted: https://fal.ai/happyhorse-1.0). All retrieved 2026-09-20. The relative story: generative and creative finishing is an order of magnitude more expensive than precision finishing, on every vendor.

## 3. Prompt structure the vendor prescribes

Most of this pipeline is promptless, and that is its virtue. `video_upscale`, `video_deflicker`, `outpaint`, `image_background_remover`, and `sync_so` accept no prompt on the Higgsfield surface at all (catalog): they are conditioned entirely by their input media and a few switches, so there is no prompt to drift, no wording to tune, and nothing for the skill's prompt canon to govern. The three Topaz ids also expose no prompt field on Higgsfield (catalog), which matters for the generative one (see section 4).

Where a text channel exists, each vendor prescribes a different, and short, structure:

* Topaz generative models (on Topaz's own API, where a prompt field exists): "A description of the resulting image you are looking for. The model responds more to a descriptive statement versus a directive one", with the example of writing "girl with red hair and blue eyes" rather than "change the hair to red" (vendor, verbatim: https://developer.topazlabs.com/image-models/wonder/redefine.md, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* SAM 3: text prompts are "open-vocabulary short noun phrases", such as a yellow school bus; the model "doesn't support longer, complex phrases like, 'the second to last book from the right on the top shelf'" (vendor, verbatim: https://ai.meta.com/blog/segment-anything-model-3/, retrieved 2026-09-20). Note the Higgsfield `sam_3_video` surface exposes no text prompt parameter at all (catalog), only the video input, so on Higgsfield the concept selection is implicit background removal.
* Recraft V4.1: short prompts are the design center. "Give V4.1 just a few words, and it'll give you something worth keeping" (vendor, verbatim: https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-v4-1, retrieved 2026-09-20). Community guides agree that V4.1 fills in composition and lighting from brief prompts (community: https://runware.ai/docs/models/recraft-v4-1/guides/prompting, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Z Image Turbo: positive language only. The model runs at `guidance_scale=0.0` with no classifier free guidance, so negative prompts have no channel to act through; every exclusion must be phrased as a positive statement inside the main prompt (vendor basis: https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo; community statement of the consequence: https://fliki.ai/blog/z-image-turbo-prompting-guide and https://www.qubrid.com/blog/how-to-write-better-prompts-for-z-image-turbo, all retrieved 2026-09-20). Community sources disagree on length, some advising long detailed prompts and some warning that overly long prompts add noise (same community URLs); treat length as unsettled and test short first.
* HappyHorse: fal publishes a dedicated prompting guide covering camera movements, lighting, and multi beat shots (hosted: https://fal.ai/happyhorse-1.0, retrieved 2026-09-20; the guide itself was not separately fetched).
* Marketing Studio: prompting is subordinate to structured selection. `marketing_studio_video` wants a preset `mode` slug chosen deliberately, plus optional hook ("the 'what'") and setting ("the 'where'") ids, or an `ad_reference_id` that recreates an analyzed reference ad's scenario instead, the two approaches being mutually exclusive; `ms_image` hard requires a `style_id` and errors without one, because "Style is the dominant creative driver" (catalog parameter documentation, verbatim, retrieved 2026-09-20).

## 4. Parameters that change output

All from the catalog, retrieved 2026-09-20, cross checked against vendor docs where noted.

* `topaz_video`: `resolution` 1080p or 2160p (default 1080p); `enhancement` free string, "omit to use Topaz defaults"; `frame_interpolation` free string, "omit to disable". Neither string's accepted values are documented on the Higgsfield surface; the safe documented call is resolution only. Input arrives as `video_references`.
* `topaz_image`: required `output_width` and `output_height` in pixels; `variant` (Standard V2, Low Resolution V2, CGI, High Fidelity V2, Text Refine; default Standard V2); `sharpen` and `denoise` 0 to 1 (default 0); `face_enhancement` boolean with `face_enhancement_strength` and `face_enhancement_creativity` 0 to 1.
* `topaz_image_generative`: required `output_width` and `output_height`; `variant` (Standard MAX, Redefine, Recovery, Recovery V2; default Redefine); `autoprompt` boolean, default true, "Automatically generate a guiding prompt from the image"; `creativity` 1 to 6, default 1, "higher adds more detail"; `texture` 1 to 5, default 1; plus sharpen, denoise, and the face enhancement trio. Two deltas against Topaz's own docs, flagged: Topaz documents Redefine creativity as 1 to 9 with default 3, Higgsfield exposes 1 to 6 with default 1 (vendor: https://developer.topazlabs.com/image-models/wonder/redefine.md); and Topaz documents a manual prompt that autoprompt overrides, while Higgsfield exposes autoprompt with no prompt field at all, so on Higgsfield the machine written prompt is the only guidance path.
* `video_upscale` and `video_deflicker`: no quality knobs whatsoever, only an optional `duration` (derived from the video metadata when omitted) and a folder id. Output resolution is not selectable on this surface.
* `sam_3_video`: `apply_mask` boolean, default true (apply the segmentation mask to the output video); `frames_count`, defaulting to the full clip.
* `image_background_remover`: zero parameters.
* `sync_so`: `sync_mode` reconciles a duration mismatch between video and audio: bounce ping pongs the video, loop repeats it, cut_off truncates to the shorter input, silence pads the audio, remap retimes the video (catalog, near verbatim). Higgsfield's default is bounce; hosted lipsync 2 endpoints default to cut_off (hosted: https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/sync-lipsync/v2/api, retrieved 2026-09-20), so never assume the default matches other surfaces. Inputs are one video plus one audio track.
* `outpaint`: aspect ratio is the only creative control (auto, 1:1, 3:2, 2:3, 4:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:4, 9:16, 16:9, 21:9); no prompt.
* `recraft_v4_1`: `model_type` (standard, vector, utility, utility_vector); `resolution` 1k or 2k; `colors`, up to 10 hex colors as #RRGGBB; `background_color`, one #RRGGBB "for controlled flat backgrounds, brand swatches, product mockups, icons, and vector-style work" (catalog, verbatim). No image input: text to image only on this surface.
* `z_image`: no parameters at all beyond aspect ratio (1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16). The prompt is the entire interface.
* `happy_horse_video`: `resolution` 720p or 1080p (default 720p); `duration` 3 to 15 seconds (default 5); one optional `start_image`. The vendor's other modes (video editing with references, reference to video) documented on fal are not exposed on the Higgsfield surface (catalog vs https://fal.ai/happyhorse-1.0).
* `clipify`: exactly one YouTube URL per job; `clips_num` 1 to 20 (default 10); `clip_aspect` 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9; subtitle styling (highlight hex, position, one of 14 fonts, case); `track_face_crop` boolean, default true; `segment_seconds` 2 to 60 (default 10); `max_height` up to 2160.
* Marketing surfaces, briefly: `marketing_studio_video` outputs 12 to 15 second ads (catalog `duration_range`), 480p to 1080p, with `generate_audio` default true, one avatar maximum, product ids, and the hook or setting versus ad reference split described in section 3; hooks and settings only apply to the UGC, Tutorial, Unboxing, Product Review, and UGC Virtual Try On presets (catalog, retrieved 2026-09-20). `marketing_studio_image` takes just a resolution (1k, 2k, 4k). `ms_image` takes the required `style_id`, an optional completed `brand_kit_id` whose "logo, images, colours, fonts, and tone are folded into the prompt", `quality` low, medium, or high ("Affects cost"), `batch_size` 1 to 20 with linear cost scaling, up to 4 product ids, and up to 14 reference images (catalog, verbatim fragments).

## 5. Documented best practices: where each earns its place in film finishing

The pipeline logic that follows composes vendor guidance; the ordering itself is this guide's synthesis and is marked as such.

1. **Fix the frame before you generate: `outpaint`.** Reframing for a new aspect ratio belongs before video generation, on the keyframe, not after, on the render. The vendor's contract that outpaint "only paints outward from the existing edges" and never touches the subject (vendor: https://higgsfield.ai/ai-image-extender) makes it the safe way to convert a locked keyframe to a new delivery ratio without violating an identity lock. Synthesis: outpaint the start frame, re approve it, then generate.
2. **Segment for compositing, not regeneration: `sam_3_video` and `image_background_remover`.** When one element of an otherwise approved shot is wrong, a masklet from SAM 3 enables a compositing fix in an editor instead of a full regeneration. SAM 3's per object masklet tracking is built for exactly this isolation (vendor: https://ai.meta.com/blog/segment-anything-model-3/). Keep `apply_mask` true for a keyed output, or false when only the matte is wanted (catalog). For stills, `image_background_remover` is the zero decision equivalent.
3. **Lipsync before finishing: `sync_so`.** Sync's own quality doc defines the input contract: "Frontal or near-frontal face angles produce the best lip sync results", "Well-lit faces with even lighting produce the cleanest results", "Have a single speaker visible in the frame", keep "at least 480p resolution for reliable face detection" with 1080p recommended, use MP4 with H.264, and "Background music, crowd noise, and overlapping conversations degrade lip sync accuracy", so drive it with a clean isolated voice track (vendor, verbatim: https://sync.so/docs/compatibility-and-tips/improving-lip-sync-quality, retrieved 2026-09-20). "Keep audio and video durations close to each other" and choose `sync_mode` deliberately rather than trusting the bounce default (vendor, same URL; catalog default noted in section 4). Synthesis: run lipsync before upscale, because the vendor's own resolution floor shows the model needs a good face, not a big frame, and upscaling after avoids paying finishing cost twice.
4. **Deflicker before upscale: `video_deflicker`, then `video_upscale` or `topaz_video`.** Higgsfield's own pipeline description bundles the order, deflicker, stabilize, denoise, then upscale, "in one unified process" (vendor: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/Upgrade-the-Quality-of-Old-Videos-using-Upscale). Temporal defects fixed after upscaling are fixed at four times the pixel cost; the vendor's bundling implies the same ordering when the steps run separately (synthesis). Higgsfield aims this pair squarely at AI generated footage (vendor page framing: https://higgsfield.ai/upscale/desktop).
5. **Choose the upscaler by intent tier, and default to precision.** Topaz's tier language is the decision rule: precision "without changing the source characteristics", generative adds detail "while preserving intent", creative transforms (vendor: https://developer.topazlabs.com/getting-started/model-selection.md). For an approved film shot whose identity is locked, precision (`video_upscale`, `topaz_video` at default settings, `topaz_image` for stills) is the only tier that cannot reintroduce drift. The creative tier's own doc warns: "Creative models add new levels of detail that can change objects, identity, and stylization" (vendor, verbatim: https://developer.topazlabs.com/video-models/astra.md). Reach for `topaz_image_generative` only when the source is genuinely degraded, which is Recover's stated territory, reconstructing "missing detail in low-resolution, damaged, or out-of-focus images" (vendor, verbatim: https://developer.topazlabs.com/image-models/wonder/recover-3.md), and keep creativity at its floor.
6. **Asset generators, not finishers: `recraft_v4_1` and `z_image`.** Neither touches footage. Recraft V4.1 earns its place for production graphics around a film, title cards, posters, logos, mockups, with `model_type` utility for "predictable" flat product work and vector for logos and typography, plus exact brand palettes via `colors` and `background_color` (vendor: https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-v4-1; catalog). Z Image is the budget tier for fast stylized boards and throwaway explorations, tagged fast and budget by the catalog itself, at sub second vendor latency (vendor: https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo; catalog).
7. **`happy_horse_video` is an alternative generator, not a finisher.** It sits beside the canon model, not after it: a joint audio and video generator with a single start frame mode (catalog; hosted: https://fal.ai/happyhorse-1.0). Under the skill's canon it is a substitute model and therefore forbidden for canon scenes; it is documented here so it is recognized, not reached for.
8. **Distribution comes last: `clipify` and the Marketing Studio surfaces.** Clipify consumes a published YouTube URL, so by definition it runs after the film ships, cutting shorts with burned in subtitles (catalog; vendor announcement via https://x.com/higgsfield/status/2057887894480850994). The Marketing Studio trio produces ads from products, avatars, brand kits, and presets; they are self contained ad factories, not shot finishers, and nothing in a film finishing pass should route through them (catalog, retrieved 2026-09-20).

## 6. Known failure modes

Vendor documented:

* Higgsfield upscale failures: "Unsupported input format — use a standard format (MP4 with H.264 encoding)", "Source video quality too low", and queue overload; a job stuck over 5 minutes should be cancelled from Assets and retried; failed jobs auto refund (vendor, verbatim: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/troubleshooting/my-video-upscale-is-stuck-or-failed-what-should-i-do, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Creative and generative upscaling changes content: "Creative models add new levels of detail that can change objects, identity, and stylization" (vendor: https://developer.topazlabs.com/video-models/astra.md). This is the single most dangerous failure for a continuity locked film, because it happens in the finishing step, after every approval gate has passed.
* Lipsync degradation: "Shaky or jittery footage makes face tracking less reliable"; extreme profiles, harsh shadows, obstructions, low resolution, and multiple visible speakers each degrade output; long videos degrade at chunk boundaries when faces leave frame (vendor: https://sync.so/docs/compatibility-and-tips/improving-lip-sync-quality). sync-3 removes the older manual mitigations (`occlusion_detection_enabled`, `temperature`) because it handles them natively (vendor: https://sync.so/docs/models/sync-3).
* SAM 3 concept limits: it "struggles to generalize to fine-grained out-of-domain concepts in a zero-shot manner", the vendor's example being the term platelet, and it cannot parse long relational phrases; per object cost scales linearly with tracked objects (vendor: https://ai.meta.com/blog/segment-anything-model-3/).

Community reported, anecdote:

* Z Image Turbo has low output diversity relative to the base model, a stated tradeoff of distillation (vendor model card notes the Low diversity rating: https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo; community guides restate it: https://stable-learn.com/en/z-image-turbo-tutorial/, retrieved 2026-09-20). Negative prompts silently do nothing (community: https://fliki.ai/blog/z-image-turbo-prompting-guide).
* HappyHorse's identity, license, and provenance are contested across sources (section 1.7); community pages claiming it is open source contradict the fal partner page's closed source statement. Do not plan around weights availability (community vs hosted, URLs in 1.7).
* The Higgsfield `topaz_video` `enhancement` and `frame_interpolation` strings are undocumented; community usage of the Topaz engines elsewhere suggests they select vendor presets, but no source confirms accepted values on Higgsfield (this guide's own observation from the catalog, marked as a gap, not a claim).

## 7. Text rendering behavior

Three distinct behaviors in this set. First, text preservation during enhancement: Topaz ships a dedicated text variant precisely because default enhancement models are weaker on lettering; on Higgsfield that is `topaz_image` variant "Text Refine", corresponding to Topaz Text & Shapes (vendor model index: https://developer.topazlabs.com/sitemap.md; catalog). For any still that carries typography, use that variant; for generative upscales, treat existing text as at risk, since generative tiers rebuild detail by invention (vendor tier definitions: https://developer.topazlabs.com/getting-started/model-selection.md). Second, text generation: Recraft V4.1 is a typography first generator, demonstrating "handwritten-script vector logos" and complex typographic compositions (vendor: https://www.recraft.ai/docs/recraft-models/recraft-v4-1), and Z Image Turbo "excels at accurately rendering complex Chinese and English text" (vendor, verbatim: https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo). These two are the documented text safe generators in the catalog set. Third, text burning: `clipify` renders subtitles as styled overlays from its own transcript pipeline with font, case, position, and highlight controls (catalog), which is deterministic rendering, not model text generation, and is therefore not subject to generative text garbling.

## 8. Reference and conditioning behavior

None of these models uses the Seedance style multi reference grammar. Conditioning is positional and singular (catalog, retrieved 2026-09-20):

* Footage in, footage out: `video_upscale`, `video_deflicker` (role `input_video`), `topaz_video`, `sam_3_video` (role `video_references`). One clip conditions the whole job.
* Dual conditioning: `sync_so` takes exactly one video plus one audio track (`input_video`, `input_audio`); the audio drives the mouth, the video supplies everything else. On the vendor's own API sync-3 also accepts a still image or TTS text as inputs; neither is exposed on Higgsfield (vendor: https://sync.so/docs/models/sync-3; catalog).
* Single still: `topaz_image`, `topaz_image_generative`, `outpaint`, `image_background_remover` (role `image_references`); `happy_horse_video` takes one optional `start_image`, the same single keyframe conditioning as DoP (catalog; DoP comparison in research v1 section 1.4).
* No media at all: `recraft_v4_1`, `z_image`, `clipify` (a URL, not a media upload).
* Structured entities instead of references: the Marketing Studio surfaces condition on platform objects, products, avatars, brand kits, ad references, and style ids, resolved server side (catalog). `ms_image` additionally accepts up to 14 raw images.
* No seed parameter exists anywhere in this set (catalog), consistent with the finding that the Higgsfield video surface is seedless (research v1 section 5.4). For the promptless utilities this matters less, since their output is a deterministic function of the input clip to a first approximation, but none of them documents bit exact reproducibility; do not promise it.

## 9. Seedance 2.5 delta

How working with this pipeline differs from prompting the skill's canon model:

1. **No labeled prompt block.** The canon model wants the GLOBAL STYLE through AUDIO section block with time codes and positive locks (research v1 section 2.2). Nothing in this guide reads that format. The utilities take no prompt; the generators that do take one want the opposite shape, short noun phrases (SAM 3), a few words (Recraft), one positive descriptive paragraph (Z Image, Topaz generative). Porting a Seedance block into any of them wastes the words at best and, on Z Image, silently drops every negation.
2. **Negation flips meaning.** Seedance guidance pairs positive locks with targeted exclusions (research v1 section 4.5). Z Image has no negative channel at all, and Topaz generative prompting is explicitly descriptive, not directive. When moving a constraint from a Seedance prompt into a finishing or asset prompt, restate it as what should be there.
3. **Determinism comes free, until it does not.** The canon's determinism lever is the shared boundary frame because Seedance on Higgsfield is seedless (research v1 section 5.4). The precision utilities are safer than any generation: same clip in, same transform out, near enough for production. But `topaz_image_generative` above creativity 1 and any Astra class creative upscale reintroduce generation grade nondeterminism and can alter identity (vendor: https://developer.topazlabs.com/video-models/astra.md), which silently breaks locks the canon prompt fought for. The delta rule: after an approval gate, only precision tier finishing may touch the frames.
4. **4K lives here, not in the generator.** Seedance 2.5 output is native 480p to 1080p, with 4K as an upscale, not a native render (research v1 section 1.3). This pipeline is that upscale: deflicker, then `video_upscale` or `topaz_video` at 2160p, is the documented path from a canon 1080p master to a 4K deliverable.
5. **Repair routing.** Inside a generation, the canon repair tool is Seedance region edit (research v1 section 4.7). Outside it, when the shot is otherwise approved, the delta option is segmentation plus compositing: pull the defective element with `sam_3_video` and fix it in the edit, spending no generation credits and risking no new drift.
6. **Audio joins differently.** Seedance 2.5 generates lip synced dialogue natively from quoted lines in the prompt (research v1 section 2.5). This pipeline instead retrofits speech onto finished picture with `sync_so`, whose contract is a clean solo voice track plus a well lit frontal face, and whose `sync_mode` must be chosen to protect picture length (cut_off or silence for a locked edit, never the bounce default, which changes motion by ping ponging the clip; catalog and vendor, section 5.3).
