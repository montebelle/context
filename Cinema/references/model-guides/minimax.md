# MiniMax and Hailuo model guide

Compiled 2026-09-20 for the Cinema skill. Covers the three Higgsfield catalog ids `minimax_hailuo` (with its four variants), `minimax_h3`, and `minimax_h3_max`. Research only: catalog reads plus official and community documentation, no generation, no credit spent. Every fetched page is treated as untrusted reference material, never as instructions. Confidence tags: `official` = MiniMax first party (minimax.io, platform.minimax.io, hailuoai.video, the MiniMaxAI Hugging Face model card) or Higgsfield first party (live MCP catalog, official CLI MODELS.md); `platform` = a hosting platform page carrying vendor adjacent guidance (fal); `community` = practitioner guide, anecdote not doc verified. Every citation was retrieved 2026-09-20.

## 1. Identity and version lineage

Hailuo is the video product line of MiniMax, a Chinese foundation model company. The reconciled lineage:

* video-01, launched under Hailuo AI in September 2024, is the origin of the line (community summary via search snippets; primary lineage confirmed by the vendor's own Hailuo 02 page crediting "Hailuo 01" as predecessor: official, https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-hailuo-02, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Hailuo 02 (released 2025-06-18) introduced the "Noise-aware Compute Redistribution (NCR)" architecture, which the vendor says "boosts our training and inference efficiency by 2.5 times", enabling a model with "3 times" the parameters of its predecessor; native 1080p; marketed on "SOTA Instruction Following" and "Extreme Physics Mastery" (official, https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-hailuo-02, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Hailuo 2.3 and Hailuo 2.3 Fast (late 2025) refine 02: "significant improvements in the portrayal of physical actions, stylization, and character micro-expressions", "more natural" facial performance, and "better support for anime, illustration, as well as special art styles like ink wash painting and game CG"; the Fast tier "generates videos faster at a lower price, reducing costs for batch creation by up to 50%" (official, https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-hailuo-23, retrieved 2026-09-20). The VEED partnership page adds hard numbers: "Generates videos in 768p or 1080p", "6 or 10 second durations", and the Fast variant "Produces 6 second clips at 768p in around 55 seconds" (official, https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-and-veed-hailuo-23-for-pro-level-ai-video, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* MiniMax H3, also marketed as Hailuo 3.0 (released 2026-07-31), is a deliberate architectural break: MiniMax "set aside the Hailuo-02 architecture, despite the significant architectural advantages it once gave us" because it "would introduce unnecessary complexity for a model built around task generalization". H3 is one omni modal transformer that "understands unified context across text, images, video, and audio" and generates video with native stereo audio "up to 15 seconds at 2K resolution" (official, https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20). Open weights under the "MiniMax H3 Community License Agreement"; the base generator is a 33B parameter dense transformer (H3-Base) fronted by H3-Context-IR preprocessing and finished by H3-Regenerate-2K in context regeneration, with two released checkpoints: FL2VA (first and last frame mode plus text to video) and Ref2VA (omni reference mode, up to 12 files) (official, https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* MiniMax H3 Max is not a bigger H3. It is "jointly released by MiniMax and fal.ai; a video generation model post-trained by fal.ai on MiniMax H3 and optimized for high-speed generation" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). fal describes it as "fal's post-trained variant of this model, tuned for stronger prompt adherence and better aesthetics" that "generates at 768p rather than 2K, and a 5-second clip renders in under 3 seconds" (platform, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20). Despite the name, Max is the speed tier and H3 proper is the quality tier.

Mapping to Higgsfield ids: `minimax_hailuo` exposes variants `minimax`, `minimax-fast`, `minimax-2.3`, `minimax-2.3-fast` (default `minimax-2.3`); `minimax_h3` and `minimax_h3_max` are separate catalog entries (official Higgsfield catalog via models_explore get, retrieved 2026-09-20). The `minimax-2.3` variants map cleanly to Hailuo 2.3 and 2.3 Fast. The bare `minimax` and `minimax-fast` variants carry no vendor version label in the catalog; given the 512, 768, 1080 resolution set and 6 or 10 second durations they behave like the Hailuo 02 tier, but that mapping is my inference, not documented. Flagged: confirm live before relying on 02 specific behavior.

## 2. Availability and cost tier on Higgsfield

All three ids are live in the Higgsfield MCP catalog as of 2026-09-20 (official Higgsfield catalog via models_explore get, retrieved 2026-09-20):

* `minimax_hailuo`, provider name Hailuo, "Natural physics, facial emotion, multiple variants". Duration 6 or 10 (default 6), resolution 512, 768, 1080 (default 768), variant as above. Media roles: start_image, end_image (single image each).
* `minimax_h3`, provider name MiniMax, "Multimodal video generation with keyframes or image/video/audio references". Duration 4 to 15 (default 5), resolution options list only "2K" (default 2K), batch_size 1 to 4, folder_id. Media roles: start_image, end_image, image_references, video_references, audio_references. Aspect ratios auto, 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16.
* `minimax_h3_max`, provider name MiniMax, "Fast text-to-video, keyframe, and multimodal-reference video generation". Duration 5 to 15 (default 5), resolution 480p or 768p (default 768p), batch_size 1 to 4. Same media roles and aspect ratios as `minimax_h3`.

Cost tier: the catalog exposes no credit prices for any of these ids, and free trial unlimited generations are not available on any of them (`unlim.available: false` on all three; official Higgsfield catalog via models_explore get, retrieved 2026-09-20). Do not invent credit numbers; the exact cost appears at generation time on the Higgsfield surface. For scale only, the vendor's own H3 product page estimates "$0.073–$0.120 per second" at 2K on its own platform, which is vendor marketing about hailuoai.video pricing, not Higgsfield pricing (official, https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20).

Catalog drift warning, same class as the Seedance 2.5 id gap in the v1 research: the official Higgsfield CLI MODELS.md documents `minimax_hailuo` fully but contains no `minimax_h3` or `minimax_h3_max` entry at all (official, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/higgsfield-ai/cli/main/MODELS.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). The live MCP catalog is ahead of the CLI schema. Trust the live catalog for the H3 ids and treat MODELS.md as authoritative only for `minimax_hailuo` constraints.

A second drift: the Higgsfield catalog lists only "2K" as a `minimax_h3` resolution, while the vendor's own API offers H3 at "768P / 2K" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). On Higgsfield, assume H3 renders at 2K only and use `minimax_h3_max` when a cheaper 768p tier is wanted.

## 3. Prompt structure the vendor prescribes

MiniMax prescribes two entirely different prompting dialects, split by model generation. Do not mix them.

### 3.1 Legacy Hailuo dialect (Hailuo 02, 2.3, 2.3 Fast; Higgsfield `minimax_hailuo`)

The load bearing vendor syntax is the bracketed camera command. The official API docs define 15 commands written in square brackets inside the prompt text: [Truck left], [Truck right], [Pan left], [Pan right], [Push in], [Pull out], [Pedestal up], [Pedestal down], [Tilt up], [Tilt down], [Zoom in], [Zoom out], [Shake], [Tracking shot], [Static shot] (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-i2v, retrieved 2026-09-20). The rules, quoted from the same doc: "Multiple commands inside one [] take effect simultaneously" with a maximum of three recommended per bracket; sequential moves are written as separate brackets in reading order ("...[Push in], then...[Push out]"); and "Free-form descriptions also work, but explicit commands yield more accurate results" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-i2v, retrieved 2026-09-20). The same command set and rules appear on the text to video task doc, which scopes them to `MiniMax-Hailuo-2.3`, `MiniMax-Hailuo-02`, `T2V-01-Director`, and `T2V-01` (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-t2v.md, retrieved 2026-09-20).

Prompt length on this generation is "up to 2000 characters" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-i2v, retrieved 2026-09-20). Two optimizer switches matter: `prompt_optimizer` defaults to `true` and rewrites your prompt before generation; the doc says set it `false` for precision control. `fast_pretreatment` (default `false`) "reduces optimization time when `prompt_optimizer` is enabled" on the two newest legacy models (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-i2v, retrieved 2026-09-20). Whether Higgsfield's `minimax_hailuo` wrapper runs the optimizer is not documented in the catalog or MODELS.md; treat optimizer state as unknown on the Higgsfield surface and flag it before any determinism sensitive work.

Community consensus on ordering for this dialect: "[Camera command] Subject + visible action + setting + light/look + continuity constraint", one continuous shot, one visible verb, and "preserve that exact syntax in your tests" for command capitalization and spelling (community, https://minimax-ai.chat/guide/hailuo-video-prompts/, retrieved 2026-09-20).

### 3.2 H3 dialect (Higgsfield `minimax_h3`, `minimax_h3_max`)

H3 drops the bracket command system in favor of natural language relational prompting. The vendor's framing: creators "describe their intent directly in natural language, rather than just entering a simple visual prompt", and reference relationships are "expressed through natural language". The canonical vendor example: "Reference the Hitchcock camera movement from Video 1, have the character in Image 2 sing, with the vocals matching Audio 3" (official, https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20). References are cited by ordinal name inside the prompt: "Image 1", "Image 2", "Video 1", "Audio 1", and the vendor's own H3 feature guide shows role assignment lines of the form "Image 1: mood, setting, film grain", "Image 2: character", "Image 3: bag" plus technical trailers like "15s, 16:9" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-prompt.md, retrieved 2026-09-20; same convention on platform, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20).

Prompt budget grows an order of magnitude: "Every request must include one non-empty text item (the prompt is required)" with a maximum of 7000 characters per text element, which the vendor's V2 API doc frames as room for full shot lists in one request (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20).

For complex jobs the vendor publishes a structured six field prompt format on the open weights model card: `subject_definitions` (key entities and references), `summary` (high level task), `retention_analysis` (how source materials are preserved or transformed), `detailed_description` (shot by shot), `overall_soundscape`, and `non_diegetic_music`. Retention is graded with named levels: visual references use `fully_preserved`, `partially_preserved`, `attribute_transfer`, or `weak_reference`; audio references use `fully_copy`, `partially_copy`, `reference`, and `weak_reference` (official, https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, retrieved 2026-09-20). This is the H3 equivalent of the Seedance labeled section block and is the format to reach for when a scene prompt outgrows one paragraph.

H3 Max adds one prompt switch H3 proper lacks: `prompt_expansion_mode` with values `disabled`, `balanced` (default), `quality` (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). The default rewrites your prompt. For canon style locked prompting, `disabled` is the deterministic choice, for the same reason `prompt_optimizer: false` is on legacy models. Whether Higgsfield exposes this switch is undocumented in the catalog; flagged.

## 4. Parameters that change output

`minimax_hailuo` (official, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/higgsfield-ai/cli/main/MODELS.md, retrieved 2026-09-20, cross checked against the live catalog):

* `--duration` 6 or 10 (default 6). `--resolution` 512, 768, 1080 (default 768). `--variant` as in section 1.
* Constraint set, quoted from MODELS.md: resolution 512 "is incompatible with end_image" and unsupported on the `minimax-2.3` and `minimax-2.3-fast` variants; resolution 1080 is "unavailable for 10 second duration"; "Start_image or end_image required unless variant is 'minimax-2.3'"; and "End_image unsupported for 'minimax-2.3' or 'minimax-2.3-fast'". The 1080 at 6 seconds only rule matches the vendor's own matrix (768P at 6s or 10s, 1080P at 6s only) (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-i2v, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Practical consequence: on the 2.3 variants there is no end frame conditioning at all. If a shot needs a locked closing frame, the legacy id cannot deliver it on the current Higgsfield surface; use `minimax_h3`.

`minimax_h3` and `minimax_h3_max` (official Higgsfield catalog via models_explore get, retrieved 2026-09-20, cross checked against official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20):

* Duration: H3 4 to 15 seconds, H3 Max 5 to 15 seconds, "integer values only" per the vendor guide (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). Frame rate 24 FPS, audio 32 kHz stereo (official, https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Resolution: H3 2K on Higgsfield (768P also exists on the vendor API, see the drift note in section 2); H3 Max 480p or 768p, 2K unsupported. fal quantifies 2K as "1440 pixels on the short edge", "roughly 3.7 megapixels on wider formats, for example 2976x1248 at 21:9" (platform, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Aspect ratio: `adaptive` (auto) plus 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16. Load bearing rule: for text to video the "ratio is required and cannot be 'adaptive'"; image driven modes are always adaptive, following the input image; reference to video defaults to adaptive (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Keyframes: first frame 0 or 1 image, last frame 0 or 1, and last_frame "must be paired with first_frame" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). Input images 256 to 5760 px, aspect between 2:5 and 5:2.
* Mode exclusivity, the sharpest constraint in the schema: "Image-to-video and reference-to-video are mutually exclusive" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). One generation gets keyframes or the reference bundle, never both. This changes shot planning: a boundary frame chained shot cannot also carry identity reference images in the same call.
* batch_size 1 to 4 on both Higgsfield H3 ids (official Higgsfield catalog, retrieved 2026-09-20). Each batch item is a spend; keep batch_size 1 under a credit gate.
* No seed parameter appears on any of the three Higgsfield ids or in the vendor V2 schema (official Higgsfield catalog and official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, both retrieved 2026-09-20). Determinism must come from keyframes and locked prompts, exactly as on the Seedance surface.
* Audio: H3 always returns audio. "Every generation returns native stereo audio: original score, dialogue, foley, and room tone timed to the cut" (platform, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20). No audio off switch is documented in the Higgsfield catalog or the vendor V2 schema (confirmed absence, both sources above, retrieved 2026-09-20). For silent work, direct the soundscape in the prompt (the six field format's `overall_soundscape` field is the lever) and plan to strip audio in post.

## 5. Documented best practices

Vendor documented (legacy dialect):

* Use explicit bracket commands over free prose for camera; combine at most three per bracket; order brackets to sequence moves (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-i2v, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Set `prompt_optimizer: false` when you need the model to execute exactly what you wrote (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-t2v.md, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* "The more specific your prompt, the better your results" (official, https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-and-veed-hailuo-23-for-pro-level-ai-video, retrieved 2026-09-20).

Vendor documented (H3 dialect):

* Assign each reference an ordinal name and a role in prose, and state the relationship between context and target: "describe the relationship between the context and the target video in words" (official, https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* For complex generations, use the six field structured format with explicit retention levels per reference (official, https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Editing is scoped by language: "Change One Thing, Keep Everything Else", with the vendor claiming "Untouched content stays stable" during targeted edits (platform quoting vendor positioning, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20; official capability claim at https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Choose the tier by need: "Use MiniMax H3 when you need 2K or the reference and editing endpoints, and H3 Max when you want speed and stronger prompt adherence" (platform, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20).

Community reported (marked):

* Prompt order for H3: "References → Retention → Scene → Timeline → Camera → Audio → Constraints" (community, https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-prompt-minimax-h3-hailuo-3-0-a-guide-for-ai-video-creators/, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Scope every reference with an exclusion clause: "Video 1 defines the hand trajectory and movement timing only. Do not copy its actor, wardrobe, setting, lighting, or camera movement" (community, same Kapwing URL, retrieved 2026-09-20). This is the same one reference one job discipline the Seedance research converged on.
* Separate subject movement from camera movement into different sentences; prefer "standard filmmaking language", quoting the guide's list verbatim, "dolly in, track left, pan right, rack focus, handheld, locked-off", over "make it cinematic" (community, same Kapwing URL, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Describe action as starting state, movement, ending state, and bind sounds to timestamps ("[6.2s] She pulls the tab open...") (community, same Kapwing URL, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Image to video on legacy models: "Spend words on motion"; do not redescribe appearance the source frame already fixes; "Describe how the subject, environment, and camera should move, plus the parts that should remain stable" (community, https://minimax-ai.chat/guide/hailuo-video-prompts/, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* A/B testing requires the optimizer off: "Set it to `false` when you need to compare two precise prompt formulations" (community, https://minimax-ai.chat/guide/hailuo-video-prompts/, retrieved 2026-09-20). Matches the canon's one variable per retry rule.

## 6. Known failure modes

Vendor documented: MiniMax's announcements document no specific failure modes or remedies; the H3 research blog concedes only general headroom, "Visual detail can still be improved in certain scenarios" and "H3's current model size leaves room for improvement across several capabilities" (official, https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20; confirmed absence of remedy documentation across https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-hailuo-23 and https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-hailuo-02, retrieved 2026-09-20). The hard parameter constraints in section 4 (512 end_image incompatibility, no end frame on 2.3 variants, mode exclusivity) are the closest thing to vendor documented failure boundaries.

Community reported (marked, anecdote):

* Bracket commands silently ignored: usually a model compatibility problem or the optimizer rewriting the prompt; fix by verifying the variant supports Director syntax and disabling `prompt_optimizer` (community, https://minimax-ai.chat/guide/hailuo-video-prompts/, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Image to video restaging the scene instead of animating it: caused by redescribing appearance; keep only motion and preservation instructions (community, https://minimax-ai.chat/guide/hailuo-video-prompts/, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* Chaos from stacked movement: "start with [Static shot], then add one movement", and "Establish each movement alone before testing a combination" (community, https://minimax-ai.chat/guide/hailuo-video-prompts/, retrieved 2026-09-20).
* H3 identity fade in wide shots: "facial identity is harder to preserve in wide shots; medium and close-ups provide more visual information", so design closer shots rather than prompting harder; very fast motion degrades geometry, so slow the action (community, https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-prompt-minimax-h3-hailuo-3-0-a-guide-for-ai-video-creators/, retrieved 2026-09-20). The guide's stated philosophy: "design around the model's failure modes instead of trying to prompt through them".

## 7. Text rendering behavior

This is a real generational split. For H3, the vendor explicitly claims strength: the model excels "at instruction following, accurate text and brand rendering" (official, https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20), and fal's model page extends the claim to "legible type, credits, subtitles, and brand marks" and "real interface work: product landing pages, game menus and HUDs" (platform, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20). The model card lists 11 stable text languages: Arabic, Chinese, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish (official, https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, retrieved 2026-09-20).

For the legacy Hailuo line, no first party source makes any text rendering claim (confirmed absence across the 02 and 2.3 announcements and the API docs cited above, retrieved 2026-09-20); treat legacy text rendering as unverified and assume the usual generated text garbling until proven otherwise.

Cinema skill note: the OASYS canon forbids all generated text, labels, and interface graphics regardless of model capability, so H3's typography strength is irrelevant to canon scenes. It cuts the other way: because H3 is good at text, it may volunteer captions or brand marks more readily, so silent scene prompts should keep the explicit "no subtitles, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no floating labels or icons, no interface" exclusion from the Seedance playbook.

## 8. Reference and conditioning behavior

`minimax_hailuo`: conditioning is keyframes only, start_image and end_image, single image each, UUID or local path ("Media inputs accept either a UUID (upload id or previous job id) or a local file path; paths are auto-uploaded") (official, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/higgsfield-ai/cli/main/MODELS.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). No multi reference slots, no `@character` style roles, no audio or video references. End frame availability by variant is the trap; see section 4.

`minimax_h3` and `minimax_h3_max`: five media roles on the Higgsfield catalog entry: start_image, end_image, image_references, video_references, audio_references (official Higgsfield catalog via models_explore get, retrieved 2026-09-20). The vendor caps: reference images "≤ 9"; reference videos "≤ 3 clips; per-clip duration [2, 15] s; total duration ≤ 15 s"; reference audio the same 3 clip and 15 second totals; and "Mixed input is capped at 12 files in total" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). File limits: video ≤ 50MB, image ≤ 30MB, audio ≤ 15MB, request body ≤ 64MB (same source). What the references carry, in the vendor's words: the model "reads identity, performance, camera movement, composition, soundscape, and editing rhythm from whatever you give it, then carries all of it through to one coherent result" (platform carrying vendor positioning, https://fal.ai/minimax-h3, retrieved 2026-09-20).

Load bearing conditioning rules to plan shots around, both from the vendor V2 schema (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20):

1. last_frame "must be paired with first_frame". A closing frame alone is invalid.
2. "Image-to-video and reference-to-video are mutually exclusive". Keyframe chaining and identity reference bundles cannot coexist in one generation. The open weights release makes the split architectural: FL2VA is the keyframe checkpoint, Ref2VA the reference checkpoint (official, https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, retrieved 2026-09-20). For a boundary chained sequence that also needs identity locking, the identity must live in the boundary frames themselves, or the sequence must accept reference mode without keyframes.

Reference discipline transfers from the Seedance findings: name each file by ordinal, give it one job, add an exclusion clause, and grade retention explicitly with the vendor's retention levels (`fully_preserved` for a character that must not drift, `attribute_transfer` for a style donor, `weak_reference` for loose inspiration) (official levels, https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3, retrieved 2026-09-20; scoping practice community, https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-prompt-minimax-h3-hailuo-3-0-a-guide-for-ai-video-creators/, retrieved 2026-09-20). The 12 file ceiling is a ceiling, not a target; the fewer matched references rule from the Seedance research has no contradicting MiniMax evidence.

## 9. Seedance 2.5 delta

How prompting the MiniMax line differs from the skill's canon model (Seedance 2.5 on Higgsfield, per docs/from-desert-to-oasys/from-desert-to-oasys-prompting-research-v1.md):

* Dialect, the biggest delta. Legacy Hailuo wants exact bracket tokens ([Push in], [Static shot]) with vendor defined spelling; Seedance 2.5 wants camera moves written as physical events (start position, path, speed, end framing) inside a labeled section block. Never carry bracket commands into a Seedance prompt or Seedance section labels into a legacy Hailuo prompt. H3 sits between: natural filmmaking language, no bracket system, with the six field structured format as the analogue of the Seedance GLOBAL STYLE through AUDIO block.
* Reference budget. Seedance 2.5 accepts up to 50 references (30 images, 10 video, 10 audio); H3 caps at 12 (9 images, 3 video, 3 audio), the same envelope as Seedance 2.0 (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation.md, retrieved 2026-09-20, versus the Seedance figures in the v1 research). Scenes budgeted against Seedance 2.5 reference counts do not port to H3 without pruning, though the fewer references discipline means well built canon scenes already fit.
* Duration ceiling. Seedance 2.5 runs 4 to 30 seconds; H3 tops out at 15, legacy Hailuo at 10. Any canon shot over 15 seconds must be split and chained on the MiniMax side.
* Keyframes versus references. On Seedance, boundary frames and reference images coexist in one generation, which the canon's shared boundary frame method depends on. On H3 they are mutually exclusive modes (section 8). This is the single most dangerous porting assumption: a canon scene that supplies @S34Boundary style keyframes plus role scoped identity references in one call cannot be expressed as one H3 generation.
* Retention vocabulary. Seedance locks identity with prose locks ("Identity and wardrobe locked to the reference", positive locks with exact counts); H3 offers named retention levels per reference, a more parametric way to say the same thing. Translate canon REFERENCE AUTHORITY lines into `retention_analysis` entries with `fully_preserved` on identity donors.
* Prompt rewriting defaults differ. Seedance on Higgsfield documents no optimizer; legacy Hailuo silently rewrites prompts unless `prompt_optimizer` is set false, and H3 Max rewrites unless `prompt_expansion_mode` is `disabled`. A canon style exact prompt is not safe on MiniMax models until the rewriter is confirmed off.
* Audio posture. Seedance exposes a generate audio toggle on hosted surfaces; H3 has no documented off switch and always ships stereo audio (section 4). Silent canon work needs a prompt level soundscape directive plus post stripping.
* Timestamp precision. The Seedance 2.5 guide drives pacing with millisecond timecodes and an EVENT TRACK; MiniMax documents no timestamp syntax for legacy models, and for H3 the community binds sounds and beats to seconds ("[6.2s] She pulls the tab open") with vendor examples staying at whole shot granularity. Expect coarser temporal control than the canon assumes; keep beats per shot low.
* Aspect ratio friction. Seedance text to video accepts auto; H3 text to video requires an explicit ratio and "cannot be 'adaptive'" (official, https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/video-generation-v2-create.md, retrieved 2026-09-20). Canon prompts that omit ratio will fail or misrender on H3 text to video.
* What carries over unchanged: no seed anywhere on either surface, so determinism lives in keyframes and frozen prompts; one variable per retry; fewer matched references; explicit exclusion clauses per reference; and the no generated text exclusion block.
